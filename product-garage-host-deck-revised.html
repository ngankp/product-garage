Clear Thinking Is Still Expensive

Modern AI tools make polished outputs trivial — decks, PRDs, mock apps in minutes.

But generating output ≠ delivering outcomes.

The bottleneck isn't the tool. It's the quality of thinking behind it:
problem framing, judgment under constraints, and knowing what questions to ask.

What Actually Matters in Product Roles

  • How you frame ambiguous problems and ask the right questions
  • Taste and judgment on value, viability, and sequencing
  • Integrating AI into your craft without outsourcing core decisions
  • Operating inside messy, real-world constraints — not toy cases

Product Garage forces the right kind of practice — honing these skills with real challenges, not templates.

How It Works

Format (Validated from MVP)

  • Duration: 3 hours (evening 6-9pm or Saturday 10am-1pm)
  • Size: 6-12 curated mid/senior PMs and product-minded builders
  • Structure: Individual work → 10-min floating SME conversations → lightweight demos + Q&A
  • Focus: Deep-dive on ONE area (solution, pricing, or GTM) with defended reasoning — 50/50 split between process/thinking narrative and output quality

What We Evaluate

  • Problem definition: Can you frame messy reality into a focused problem?
  • Prioritization & trade-offs: Must-have vs nice-to-have, and why?
  • Business impact: Connect decisions to value and viability
  • Use of AI: Where does AI accelerate? Where do you hold the line on judgment?
