## About Product Garage

Product Garage is a co-founded PM event series focused on helping mid/senior PMs hone product craft through deep-dive workshops on real business challenges.

**Format:**
- 3-hour workshops (evening or Saturday morning)
- 6-12 curated participants
- Individual work with SME access
- Real product challenges from partner companies
- Expert feedback from product leaders

**Partnership Opportunities:**
- **Idea Host**: Provide a real product challenge + SME access
- **Venue Host**: Provide space + AV + refreshments
